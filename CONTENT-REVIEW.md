# Editorial decisions

Reviewed the rendered homepage and all five project routes before editing, alongside their bilingual content and shared components.

- **Keep:** visual hierarchy, section order, project order, real screenshots, contact links, resume, education and languages. The short engineering headline and AI review principle already communicate the intended identity.
- **Hero / About:** establish the medical-device context immediately; use About to explain how evidence, processes and business requirements shape the work rather than repeat technologies.
- **Experience:** five concise contributions cover cross-functional DQA, investigation support, approved-procedure testing, controlled records and actual tools delivered. Link to the existing professional case study for depth.
- **Skills:** preserve relevant languages and tools while moving duplicated architecture concepts to their existing panel. Qualify academic/practical database experience, basic statistical concepts, metrology exposure and deployment fundamentals.
- **AI:** explain actual inspect–plan–test–iterate workflows and give AI Video Editor as evidence, replacing a repeated use-case list.
- **Projects:** retain confirmed functionality and technologies. Strengthen the distinction between entrepreneurship (KREA), reliability (video editor), persistent application data (FORGE), and requirements-led client work (NAIA). Do not infer further features from screenshots.
- **Professional case:** group testing/validation, investigations/records, reporting and onboarding into short sections and bullets. Explicitly distinguish related contributions from a single implemented pipeline and support work from final ownership or approval.
- **Omit deliberately:** manufacturing station counts, shipment destinations and detailed measurement operations. External sterilization preparation and routine collaboration applications are valid context but unnecessary for the selected public narrative. No confidential employer documents, identifiers, patient information or internal results are included.
